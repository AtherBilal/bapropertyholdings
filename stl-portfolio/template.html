How to read these numbers

  • Period: November 1, 2025 to October 31, 2026. Stays crossing a month or period boundary are split by night.
  • Source: payout records from our booking system. Not audited.
  • Cleaning: actual cost per clean, by unit size, times the number of stays.
  • Utilities: our typical monthly cost: about $125 for a 1-bedroom, $175–200 for the larger units. Bills available on request.
  • Not included: supplies and consumables, insurance, software, guest messaging and management.
  • Leases: rents shown are current monthly rents. Lease terms and any transfer are subject to each landlord's approval and are shared on request.
